01 — THE CHALLENGE

Local problems deserve visible solutions.

Citizens experience problems in their communities every day. The challenge is turning those individual voices into organized information that can guide effective action.

01

Scattered Complaints

Community problems can be reported through different channels, making them difficult to organize and monitor.

02

Limited Visibility

Citizens may not always know whether their complaint has been received, reviewed or acted upon.

03

Difficult Prioritization

Authorities need a clearer picture of which community issues require urgent attention.
